Outlook / Hotmail accounts

No Azure setup needed — forward them into Gmail instead:

  1. Open outlook.com, sign into the Hotmail account → ⚙ Settings → Mail → Forwarding
  2. Turn on Enable forwarding, enter your Gmail address, save. Tick "keep a copy" if you like.
  3. Mail now lands in Gmail — and in this hub. To reply as the Hotmail address: in Gmail → Settings → Accounts → "Send mail as" → add the address.
